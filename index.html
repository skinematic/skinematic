What is Paramedical
Tattooing/Scar and Stretch
Mark Camouflage?

Paramedical tattooing also known as Scar and
stretch mark Camouflage is a cosmetic treatment
that uses a tattoo gun to insert an inkless DA-1
Collagen serum that heals and conceals
unwanted scars/stretch marks. Unlike traditional
tattooing we aren’t just trying to cover up
unwanted scarring, we are healing the skin.
